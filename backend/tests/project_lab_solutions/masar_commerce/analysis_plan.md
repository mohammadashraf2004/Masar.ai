# Analysis Plan

## Objective

Explain how Masar Commerce performed during 2025 and why sales changed, so that management can decide where to
invest in categories, regions and customer retention next year.

## Stakeholders

- CEO: needs the overall picture of revenue, growth and the main risks to set the priorities for next year.
- Head of Sales: needs to know which categories, products and regions bring in revenue to set targets.
- Head of Marketing: needs to understand repeat buying and customer value to plan retention campaigns.

## Business questions

- How much recognised revenue did we make in 2025, from how many orders?
- How did revenue move month by month, and is the business growing?
- How many customers come back, and who are the most valuable?
- Which categories and products drive revenue, and which never sell?
- How do the four regions compare?

## KPI definitions

Recognised revenue is the sum of quantity times unit_price over the lines of completed orders only; cancelled,
returned and pending orders are excluded. Average order value (AOV) is recognised revenue divided by the number of
completed orders. Purchasing customers are customers with at least one completed order.
