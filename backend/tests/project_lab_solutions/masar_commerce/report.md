# Executive Summary

Masar Commerce grew through 2025: the second half brought in clearly more revenue than the first, with a strong
November. Revenue depends heavily on Electronics and on the Egypt and Gulf regions, and almost half of our buyers
never came back after their first order. Cancelled, returned and pending orders hold a large amount of value.

# Business Performance

Masar Commerce recognised 11.47 million EGP of revenue from 3,357 completed orders in 2025, an average order value
of 3,417 EGP. About 1,227 customers bought at least once.

# Customer Insights

The repeat customer rate is 53.8%: just over half of purchasing customers ordered twice or more. Business customers
place much larger orders than consumers. The top ten customers bring in about 5% of revenue, so we are not dependent
on a few accounts.

# Product Insights

Electronics is the leading category with about 41% of revenue, followed by Home & Kitchen. Two products never sold
at all and should be reviewed. Fashion carries the deepest discounts.

# Regional and Time Trends

Revenue peaked in November, helped by White Friday, and the second half of the year grew about 23% over the first.
Egypt is our largest region by revenue, while Gulf customers place the most valuable orders.

![Monthly revenue](charts/monthly_revenue.png)

The year ends far stronger than it started.

![Revenue by region](charts/revenue_by_region.png)

Egypt and the Gulf bring in most of the money.

# Key Risks

- Concentration: Electronics and two regions carry most of the revenue.
- Leakage: cancelled, returned and pending orders hold over 2.8 million EGP of value.
- Retention: almost half of buyers ordered only once.

# Recommendations

- Marketing: launch a second-purchase offer for one-time customers within 30 days of their first order.
- Sales: grow Home & Kitchen and Beauty to reduce dependence on Electronics.
- Operations: investigate the causes of cancellations and returns, starting with the largest categories.
